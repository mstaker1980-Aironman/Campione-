Scopes of Work

What we install

Full-scope commercial flooring — from demolition and substrate prep through final transitions and closeout documentation. One sub, one schedule, one point of contact.

Carpet Tile & Broadloom

Modular carpet tile, walk-off systems, and broadloom for corporate, education, and hospitality interiors.

Luxury Vinyl & Resilient

LVT and LVP, sheet vinyl, rubber tile, and homogeneous sheet goods with heat-welded seams.

Ceramic, Porcelain & Stone

Floor and wall tile, large-format porcelain, waterproofing and crack-isolation membranes.

Polished Concrete

Grind, densify, and polish to specified gloss and clarity. Dyes, joint fill, and guard systems.

Resinous & Epoxy Systems

Epoxy, urethane cement, and MMA systems for kitchens, labs, and industrial environments.

Floor Prep & Demolition

Selective demo, shot blasting, grinding, self-leveling underlayment, and substrate remediation.

Moisture Mitigation

RH testing per ASTM F2170, calcium chloride testing, and vapor-barrier systems on slab.

Base, Trim & Transitions

Rubber and vinyl wall base, stair treads and nosings, reducers, and ADA-compliant transitions.

For General Contractors

Why GCs keep us on the list

Flooring lands at the end of the schedule, when the float is gone and the certificate of occupancy is the only thing anyone cares about. That's the part we're built for.

We hold the date

Flooring is the last trade in and the first one blamed. We staff to the schedule we signed, including nights and weekends when the turnover date demands it.

Submittals that don't bounce

Complete submittal packages, product data, and samples returned on time — not three RFIs later.

Substrate problems caught early

Moisture testing before material hits the floor. Finding a failing slab in week two is a schedule item; finding it after install is a claim.

Short punch lists

We walk our own work before the architect does, and we close out with the O&M manuals and warranties in hand.

Occupied-space experience

Phasing, containment, and after-hours work in hospitals, schools, and offices that never actually close.

Local, and staying that way

North Kansas City since 2008. Our crews live here, our references are down the road, and we'll still be here for the warranty call.

05
Public Procurement

Kansas Statewide Contract

Floor Coverings — Furnish and Install · Contract 56954A

Country Carpet holds a statewide contract with the Kansas Department of Administration for commercial floor coverings, furnished and installed. Agencies can purchase directly off it without running a separate competitive bid.

Who can use it

State agencies, and political subdivisions of the State of Kansas — cities, counties, school districts, and universities. Pricing is available to all of them at contract rates.

What's covered

Broadloom carpet · carpet tile · VCT · LVT · Marmoleum and rubber · sheet vinyl · porcelain and ceramic tile · adhesives. Furnish and install, with pricing on both material and labor.

How to use it

Contract 56954A, awarded by the Office of Facilities and Property Management. Current terms and pricing are listed on the Kansas eSupplier portal. Payment terms are Net 30; P-Cards are not accepted on this contract.

Questions about scope, scheduling, or a specific project — call Maple Hill at 785.256.4800.

Our Story

Built in North Kansas City, one floor at a time

Campione Interior Solutions was founded in 2008 and has worked out of North Kansas City ever since. What started as a small crew has grown into a commercial flooring subcontractor trusted on projects across the metro — but the way the work gets done hasn't changed much: show up when we said we would, install it right the first time, and stand behind it after the building opens.

Eighteen years in, most of our work still comes from general contractors we've worked with before — and from the ones they tell about us.
